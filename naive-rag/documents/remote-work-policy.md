# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role is designated hybrid. Teams choose two anchor days each week when all local members are expected to attend the office.

Working from another country requires written approval from People Operations and Tax at least 30 days before travel. Approval is normally limited to 20 working days in a rolling 12-month period.

Remote workers must use company-managed devices, connect through the approved VPN on public networks, and keep confidential conversations away from shared public spaces.
