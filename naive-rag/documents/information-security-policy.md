# Information Security Policy

All employees must use multi-factor authentication for company email, source control, finance, and administrative systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across company and personal accounts.

A suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations channel within 30 minutes of discovery. Employees should not investigate compromised devices themselves unless directed by the incident commander.

Restricted data may be stored only in approved company systems. Copying customer records to personal cloud storage, removable media, or unapproved AI services is prohibited.
