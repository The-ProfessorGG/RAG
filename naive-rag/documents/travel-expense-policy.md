# Travel and Expense Policy

Employees should book economy airfare for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights lasting six hours or longer with manager approval. Business class requires executive approval before purchase.

Hotel costs are reimbursable up to 2,500 rand per night in South Africa and up to 180 US dollars per night elsewhere. Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense above 300 rand.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Finance may reject late claims unless the employee includes a manager-approved explanation.
