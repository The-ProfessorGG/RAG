# Equipment and Device Policy

Northstar Labs provides each employee with one primary laptop and the standard accessories needed for their role. Monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic equipment may be requested through the service desk.

Company equipment remains the property of Northstar Labs and must be returned within five business days after employment ends. Loss or theft must be reported to the service desk and the employee's manager within 24 hours.

Employees may not install unlicensed software or disable endpoint-security controls. Personal use is permitted when it is limited, lawful, and does not interfere with work or expose company data.
