# Parental Leave Policy

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave may begin up to two weeks before the expected arrival date.

Employees should notify People Operations at least eight weeks before the expected start date when reasonably possible. The company may accept shorter notice for adoption timing, medical changes, or emergencies.

Up to four weeks of parental leave may be taken flexibly during the first six months after the child's arrival when the employee and manager agree on the schedule in writing.
