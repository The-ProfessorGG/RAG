# Customer Refund Policy

Standard subscriptions may be refunded within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase when the customer has used fewer than five hours of the service. Renewal charges are not automatically refundable.

Duplicate charges and confirmed billing errors must be refunded in full. Support agents may approve refunds up to 2,000 rand; larger refunds require approval from a support manager.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and normally appear within five to ten business days after approval.
