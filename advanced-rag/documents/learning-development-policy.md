# Learning and Development Policy

Each permanent employee receives an annual learning budget of 12,000 rand. The budget may be used for courses, books, certification exams, and professional conferences related to the employee's current role or agreed development plan.

Manager approval is required before committing company funds. Any single request above 6,000 rand also requires approval from the department director.

Employees who pass an approved certification exam may claim the exam fee. Travel and accommodation for a conference are charged to the travel budget, not the learning budget.
