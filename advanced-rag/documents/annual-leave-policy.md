# Annual Leave Policy

Northstar Labs grants full-time employees 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly from the employee's start date. Part-time employees receive a proportional allowance based on contracted hours.

Employees may carry over no more than 5 unused annual-leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by 31 March or it expires unless the People Director approves an exception in writing.

Leave requests of five consecutive working days or more should be submitted at least four weeks in advance. Managers may decline a requested period when critical operational coverage cannot be maintained, but they must suggest reasonable alternatives.
